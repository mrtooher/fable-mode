SPEC:
Current coffee service cost: $0.50 per cup (complimentary).
Daily cup usage: 150 cups.
Proposed premium machine lease: $400/month.
Premium bean cost: $0.80 per cup.
Proposed member price for premium coffee: $3.00 per cup.
Expected daily premium cup sales: 80 cups.
Remaining free cups: 70 per day.
Installation and plumbing cost: $1,200 (one-time).
Contract length: 24 months.
To: Managing Committee
From: Jim, Beverage Director
Date: November 22, 2026
Subject: Main Bar Premium Coffee Service Upgrade
I recommend we upgrade our main bar coffee service to a premium automated machine, replacing our current complimentary drip coffee setup. This upgrade addresses member feedback regarding coffee quality while improving the department's overall financial performance.
Currently, we provide 150 complimentary cups daily at $0.50 each, costing the club $75 per day. While this service is appreciated, it represents a pure cost center with no revenue offset. Under the proposed model, we expect 80 members to switch to the premium coffee at $3.00 per cup, while 70 cups remain complimentary for those who prefer the standard offering.
The premium coffee generates $240 in daily revenue (80 cups x $3.00) against $64 in daily bean costs (80 cups x $0.80), yielding $176 in daily gross profit. Over a 30-day month, this is $5,280. Subtracting the $400 monthly machine lease leaves $4,880 in monthly net profit from the paid coffee.
Additionally, the shift reduces our complimentary coffee volume from 150 cups to 70 cups. This saves $40 per day ($0.50 x 80 cups), or $1,200 per month. Combined with the paid coffee profit, the total monthly financial benefit is $6,080. The $1,200 one-time installation cost will be recovered in the first week of operation.
Member satisfaction with the main bar experience is a top priority. Recent surveys indicated that while our food and cocktail programs score highly, the coffee service is perceived as dated. By introducing a premium bean sourced from a local roaster, we elevate the morning and post-dinner experience. The $3.00 price point was benchmarked against local cafes and is well within the range our members are accustomed to paying for quality coffee.
The proposed machine is a fully automated super-automatic espresso system, which requires minimal barista training and ensures consistent quality across all shifts. The 24-month contract includes all maintenance and parts, eliminating unexpected repair costs. Our current bar layout has adequate counter space and existing plumbing within 10 feet of the proposed location, which keeps the installation scope minimal.
The transition will take place over a single weekend to minimize disruption. The beverage team has already identified the specific bean blend and conducted taste tests with a sample group of members, who responded very positively. The automated nature of the machine means our staff can focus on member interaction rather than manual brewing, improving service speed during the busy morning rush.
The strongest case against the upgrade is the $1,200 upfront plumbing cost and the risk that fewer than 80 members switch to the paid option, which would delay the payback period and reduce the projected monthly benefit. To address this, we will run a two-week promotional period offering the premium coffee at $2.00 to encourage trial and build the habit before transitioning to the full $3.00 price.
