BLOCK A - MEMOS
Memo X
SPEC:
Current tap beer cost: $110/keg (half-barrel, 124 pints).
Current tap beer price: $8/pint.
Current monthly tap volume: 20 kegs.
New distributor (LocalHops) cost: $130/keg.
LocalHops requires a minimum order of 4 kegs per delivery.
LocalHops delivers bi-weekly.
Current distributor delivers weekly.
Club cellar tap cooler capacity: 12 kegs total.
LocalHops offers a 5% rebate on total quarterly purchases over $5,000.
To: Managing Committee
From: Jim, Beverage Director
Date: November 22, 2026
Subject: Transition to LocalHops Craft Beer Distributor
I recommend we transition our main bar tap account to LocalHops, effective at the start of next quarter. Over the past six months, I have evaluated several regional distributors, and LocalHops has emerged as the clear choice for our club's evolving beverage program. Their local sourcing clearly shows our members will prefer the fresher product, driving a measurable increase in tap sales. This aligns with our strategic initiative to highlight regional partners and elevate the main bar experience.
Financially, the move is sound and supports our margin targets. At 20 kegs a month, our quarterly spend at the new $130 cost is $2,600, which easily clears LocalHops' $5,000 quarterly rebate threshold. While the per-keg cost rises by $20 compared to our current supplier, the 5% rebate on that spend will offset a portion of the increase. Furthermore, because LocalHops delivers bi-weekly, we will only need to receive 2 kegs per delivery to meet our monthly demand, simplifying our receiving process and reducing the administrative burden on the bar staff. The reduction in delivery frequency also means fewer interruptions during peak service hours.
The rebate structure is particularly attractive. LocalHops applies the 5% rebate as a credit on the following month's invoice, meaning the cash flow benefit is realized immediately. Based on our volume, this credit will effectively reduce the net cost per keg to $123.50, bringing it much closer to our current pricing while delivering a superior product.
Operationally, the transition will be seamless. LocalHops provides their own tap lines and faucets at no additional cost, and their technical team will handle the initial installation and calibration. Our bar team has already completed a training session with their representatives to ensure proper pour techniques and line maintenance.
The strongest case against switching is the $20 per keg price increase, which slightly compresses our pour cost margin compared to our current distributor. However, the marketing value of local craft beer and the anticipated increase in volume outweigh this minor margin compression. I am confident that the enhanced member experience will more than justify the slight increase in cost.
Memo Y
SPEC:
Current coffee service cost: $0.50 per cup (complimentary).
Daily cup usage: 150 cups.
Proposed premium machine lease: $400/month.
Premium bean cost: $0.80 per cup.
Proposed member price for premium coffee: $3.00 per cup.
Expected daily premium cup sales: 80 cups.
Remaining free cups: 70 per day.
Installation and plumbing cost: $1,200 (one-time).
Contract length: 24 months.
To: Managing Committee
From: Jim, Beverage Director
Date: November 22, 2026
Subject: Main Bar Premium Coffee Service Upgrade
I recommend we upgrade our main bar coffee service to a premium automated machine, replacing our current complimentary drip coffee setup. This upgrade addresses member feedback regarding coffee quality while improving the department's overall financial performance.
Currently, we provide 150 complimentary cups daily at $0.50 each, costing the club $75 per day. While this service is appreciated, it represents a pure cost center with no revenue offset. Under the proposed model, we expect 80 members to switch to the premium coffee at $3.00 per cup, while 70 cups remain complimentary for those who prefer the standard offering.
The premium coffee generates $240 in daily revenue (80 cups x $3.00) against $64 in daily bean costs (80 cups x $0.80), yielding $176 in daily gross profit. Over a 30-day month, this is $5,280. Subtracting the $400 monthly machine lease leaves $4,880 in monthly net profit from the paid coffee.
Additionally, the shift reduces our complimentary coffee volume from 150 cups to 70 cups. This saves $40 per day ($0.50 x 80 cups), or $1,200 per month. Combined with the paid coffee profit, the total monthly financial benefit is $6,080. The $1,200 one-time installation cost will be recovered in the first week of operation.
Member satisfaction with the main bar experience is a top priority. Recent surveys indicated that while our food and cocktail programs score highly, the coffee service is perceived as dated. By introducing a premium bean sourced from a local roaster, we elevate the morning and post-dinner experience. The $3.00 price point was benchmarked against local cafes and is well within the range our members are accustomed to paying for quality coffee.
The proposed machine is a fully automated super-automatic espresso system, which requires minimal barista training and ensures consistent quality across all shifts. The 24-month contract includes all maintenance and parts, eliminating unexpected repair costs. Our current bar layout has adequate counter space and existing plumbing within 10 feet of the proposed location, which keeps the installation scope minimal.
The transition will take place over a single weekend to minimize disruption. The beverage team has already identified the specific bean blend and conducted taste tests with a sample group of members, who responded very positively. The automated nature of the machine means our staff can focus on member interaction rather than manual brewing, improving service speed during the busy morning rush.
The strongest case against the upgrade is the $1,200 upfront plumbing cost and the risk that fewer than 80 members switch to the paid option, which would delay the payback period and reduce the projected monthly benefit. To address this, we will run a two-week promotional period offering the premium coffee at $2.00 to encourage trial and build the habit before transitioning to the full $3.00 price.
Memo Z
SPEC:
Program fee: $500/year per locker.
Target initial sales: 20 lockers.
Club has 400 active members.
Minimum bottle purchase to fill locker: 3 bottles.
Average bottle price for program: $150.
Club cost for program bottles: $100.
Lockers require a dedicated 10x10 ft room.
Room renovation cost: $15,000.
Projected annual maintenance for room: $1,200.
Current available space: a 10x10 ft room currently used for dry storage.
To: Managing Committee
From: Jim, Beverage Director
Date: November 22, 2026
Subject: Premium Whiskey Lockers Program
I recommend we launch the Premium Whiskey Lockers program for our active members. This initiative will provide a high-touch, exclusive amenity that aligns with our club's commitment to premium member experiences. The exclusivity of the program will clearly ensure high retention among our top-tier members, making it a strategic win for the club's long-term membership goals.
The financials support the initial capital investment. With 20 lockers at $500 each, the annual fee revenue is $10,000. Combined with the projected $3,000 in bottle margin (20 lockers x 3 bottles x $50 margin), the first-year gross program profit is $13,000. After subtracting the $15,000 renovation and $1,200 annual maintenance, the first-year net profit is $1,200. By year two, with the renovation cost fully amortized, the program will generate $11,800 in pure annual profit, providing a strong return on investment.
Over the past year, several members have requested a secure location to store their personal spirits. Currently, members must take their bottles home after each visit, which limits their ability to host guests or enjoy a quick nightcap in the lounge. The lockers will solve this pain point while generating a new, high-margin revenue stream. The $150 average bottle price point was selected based on our current top-selling premium spirits, ensuring the inventory will turn quickly and remain fresh.
Operationally, we have identified a 10x10 ft room currently used for dry storage. Because the space is already available, members can purchase a single bottle of their choice to initially stock their locker, allowing for immediate rollout without capital delays. The renovation will include custom mahogany lockers, dedicated ambient lighting, and a secure access system integrated with our existing member ID fobs.
Security and access control are paramount. The room will feature a keypad entry system tied to the member's account, and all access will be logged. The beverage staff will conduct a weekly inventory audit of the lockers to ensure compliance with club policies and to identify any bottles that have been open for an extended period.
The strongest case against the program is that some members may find the $500 annual fee too steep, which could slow the initial rollout of the 20 lockers. To mitigate this, we will offer a founding member discount of 10% for the first year to those who commit during the launch month.
