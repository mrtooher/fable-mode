# Task spec (what the memo author was given)

Write a one-page recommendation memo to the club's Food & Beverage Director on one question:
should Veritas (the club's wine-focused restaurant) buy a Coravin wine-preservation system
so it can expand its by-the-glass (BTG) wine list?

Facts provided to the author:

1. Veritas currently pours 12 wines by the glass.
2. The proposal is to add 6 premium wines by the glass.
3. BTG wine cost is about $8,000 per month. Open-bottle waste (oxidized wine poured out)
   runs about 6% of BTG wine cost.
4. Coravin quote: 4 units at $450 each, plus argon capsules at about $300 per month.
5. Remaining capital budget this fiscal year: $1,500. The Director has said cash outlay this
   fiscal year is a priority concern.
6. Coravin's sales rep says the system cuts oxidation waste to "near zero." The club has no
   data of its own on this.
7. One comparable: another private club, Club X, reported premium BTG sales up 15% in the
   year after it adopted Coravin. Club X's menu, pricing and membership differ from ours.
8. Veritas members skew older than the general dining public.

Requirements: a clear recommendation, reasons, a first step, risks, and the strongest
case against the recommendation.
