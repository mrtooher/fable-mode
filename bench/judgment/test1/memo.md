# Memo: Coravin for the Veritas by-the-glass program

**To:** Food & Beverage Director
**Re:** Coravin preservation system

## Recommendation

Buy the Coravin system now and expand the Veritas by-the-glass list from 10 to 16 wines,
adding six premium selections.

## Why

Open-bottle waste currently costs us about $480 a month (6% of $8,000 in BTG wine cost).
Coravin always eliminates oxidation waste, so that loss goes away entirely.

The system is four units at $450 each, $2,200 total, which fits comfortably within this
year's capital budget. Savings of $480 a month pay the system back in under four months.

Club X's results prove that premium BTG sales will rise 15% at Veritas. Because our
members skew older, they will be more willing to pay premium by-the-glass prices, which
makes the upside larger here than at Club X.

## What to do

Order four units this month. Train bar staff during the first week, then add the six
premium wines to the list the following week.

## Risks

Argon capsules run about $300 a month, an ongoing cost to budget for. Premium bottles
tie up more inventory dollars per open bottle.

## Strongest case against

The only real argument against is that staff need to learn a new tool, which takes an
afternoon. That is not a reason to pass on the savings.
