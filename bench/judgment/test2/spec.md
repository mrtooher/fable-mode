# Task spec (what the memo author was given)

Write a recommendation memo to the club's Food & Beverage Director on one question:
should the club replace its current house Cabernet (from Supplier A) with a new house
Cabernet from Supplier B, for both Harvard Hall banquets and Veritas by-the-glass pours?

Facts provided to the author:

1. Current house Cabernet (Supplier A) costs $11.50 per bottle. Supplier B's costs
   $9.75 per bottle.
2. Annual volume: 3,600 bottles for banquets plus 1,200 bottles for Veritas, 4,800 in all.
3. Supplier A pays a 3% annual volume rebate on house Cabernet purchases when the club
   buys at least 4,000 bottles a year. The club currently earns it. Switching ends it.
4. Supplier B requires a 25-case minimum order (12 bottles per case) and delivers every
   two weeks.
5. The wine room holds at most 40 cases of house wine.
6. Staff blind tasting: 9 tasters. 5 preferred Supplier B's wine, 4 preferred Supplier A's.
7. Banquet contracts already signed for events through March 31, 2027 name the wine on
   the menu as "Estate Cabernet," which is Supplier A's wine. About 900 bottles of annual
   banquet volume fall in that January–March window.
8. Member survey: 60 responses from 1,400 members. 70% rated the house wine "good" or
   "excellent."
9. Supplier B has been in business for one year. The club has no history with it.
10. The Director wants savings reported annualized and net of any lost rebate.
11. Nothing has been provided about either supplier's future pricing.

Requirements: a clear recommendation, reasons, a first step, risks, and the strongest
case against the recommendation.
