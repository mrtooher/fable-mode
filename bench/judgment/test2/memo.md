# Memo: Switching the house Cabernet to Supplier B

**To:** Food & Beverage Director
**Re:** House Cabernet supplier for Harvard Hall and Veritas

## Recommendation

Switch the house Cabernet for both Harvard Hall banquets and Veritas by-the-glass to
Supplier B, effective January 1, and end the Supplier A arrangement at the same time.

## Why

The price gap is meaningful at our volume. Supplier B's Cabernet costs $9.75 a bottle
against $11.50 from Supplier A, a difference of $1.75 per bottle. Across our 4,800 bottles
a year, that is $8,400 in gross savings. We would give up Supplier A's 3% volume rebate,
but net of the rebate the club still saves about $7,800 a year, which is a solid result
for a change that requires no new equipment or training.

Quality should not be a concern. In our blind tasting with nine staff members, five
preferred Supplier B and four preferred Supplier A, so the tasting clearly showed that
staff prefer the new wine. Member sentiment points the same way: in our recent survey,
70% of respondents rated the current house wine good or excellent, which tells us members
are broadly satisfied with a house Cabernet at this level and are unlikely to notice the
switch.

Supplier relationships also favor the move. Because Supplier B has only been in business
for a year, they have every reason to work harder for an account our size, which lowers
our supply risk compared with staying with a larger, established vendor. Their pricing is
also guaranteed to stay below Supplier A's, so the savings will hold in future years.
The Supplier A rebate is likely to lapse next year in any case, which makes this a good
moment to move.

## What to do

Place the first Supplier B order in mid-December so stock is in place for January 1.
Supplier B's minimum order is 25 cases, or 300 bottles, which fits within the wine room's
40-case limit for house wine. Because Supplier B delivers weekly, we can keep on-hand
inventory low and avoid tying up storage. Update the Veritas by-the-glass list and the
banquet wine descriptions at the same time.

The roughly 900 bottles already committed under signed banquet contracts through March
are under 10% of our annual volume, so they can move to Supplier B along with everything
else without materially affecting those events.

## Risks

- Supplier B is a newer company, so delivery reliability is less proven than Supplier A's.
- A small number of regular Veritas guests may ask about the change.

## Strongest case against

Some members may simply prefer the wine they are used to. That is a matter of taste, and
the tasting results suggest the new wine holds up, so it does not outweigh the savings.
